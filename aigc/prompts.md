# 实际提示词与调整记录

以下为本项目调用 Codex 内置图像生成工具时使用的完整英文提示词。三次调用各得到一张最终图片，未将静态图片称为视频，也未宣称经过多轮筛选。

## 01 / Glass Flower

> Create a single museum-quality editorial still-life artwork for a premium AI image creation portfolio. Subject: one intricate, sculptural flower made of translucent smoky glass, suspended in a near-black charcoal studio. Subtle acid-lime (#e8ed60) illumination refracts through a few petals; precise realistic caustics and soft long shadows. Strong asymmetric composition with the flower slightly left of center and generous negative space. Real tactile glass, restrained cinematic lighting, photographed with a high-end medium-format camera, crisp but not over-sharpened, sophisticated subtle film grain. No typography, no letters, no logo, no interface, no border, no collage. Square 1:1 image.

**检查：**花瓣形体清晰，光线成为视觉重心；保留为系列第一张。

## 02 / Amber Object

> Create one square museum-quality editorial product still life in the same visual series as a smoky translucent glass flower illuminated by acid-lime light in a near-black studio. Subject: a single unlabeled amber glass perfume bottle with an unusual monolithic silhouette, standing on a honed black stone plinth. A narrow chartreuse beam passes through the bottle, making physically plausible caustics and a long precise shadow. Asymmetric composition, bottle slightly right of center, generous negative space, tactile glass and stone, restrained color palette (charcoal, amber, pale acid-lime), medium-format photography, sophisticated grain. No lettering, no logos, no people, no props, no border, no collage.

**检查：**产品轮廓与石台构成稳定的几何关系；作为第二张，增加物件尺度的变化。

## 03 / Silent Arch

> A square high-end editorial architectural photograph completing a three-image series with a smoky glass flower and an amber perfume bottle. Subject: one monumental translucent smoked-glass arch standing alone in a near-black concrete room. A narrow acid-lime light slit crosses the arch and creates believable refracted light and precise shadow on the dark floor. Unusual but physically coherent structure, quiet atmosphere, highly restrained charcoal and pale chartreuse palette, asymmetric framing with generous negative space, tactile glass and concrete, medium-format photography, nuanced dark tones, subtle film grain. No people, no letters, no logo, no clutter, no border, no collage.

**检查：**拱门把相同材质放大到空间尺度；作为收束画面。

## 可以复用的提示词结构

`主体 + 场景 + 材质 + 光线 + 构图 + 摄影语言 + 排除项`

这只是写提示词的框架。真正决定系列感的是固定色彩、材质与光源，随后有意识地改变主体尺度。页面排版、标题、标签都在网页中完成，不交给图像模型生成。
